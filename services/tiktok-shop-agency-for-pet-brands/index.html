What pet brands need to make TikTok Shop work

Pet buyers often want to see the product in a real context: how it fits into a routine, what problem it is designed to solve, and whether it makes sense for a particular pet owner. That makes creator relevance, clear product information, and a simple buying path more important than generic influencer volume.

How Avanta approaches pet-brand growth

Creator and product fit

Recruit creators who can make the product context feel natural to their audience, then keep outreach, samples, approvals, and follow-up moving.

Content with a job to do

Build briefs around use, setup, routine, or product education while leaving space for credible creator execution. The brand remains responsible for claims and approvals.

Conversion after the video

Make product information, variants, bundles, and offers easy to understand after the creator sends a shopper to TikTok Shop.

Operating feedback loop

Use creator and shop performance to decide which products and angles deserve more attention, content volume, or paid amplification.
