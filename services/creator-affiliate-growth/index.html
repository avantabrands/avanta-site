Affiliate management is the work between a sample request and repeatable GMV

Brands often think they have a creator problem when they really have an operating problem. Creators are not sourced consistently, the offer is unclear, samples move slowly, briefs are vague, and no one follows up once a creator shows interest. The result is low output and even lower repeatability.

A managed program treats each stage as a system: who is recruited, how the product is positioned, what happens after a sample is accepted, which creators need support, and where the brand should concentrate its attention after content goes live.

Creator recruitment and fit

Build a creator target list around category relevance, audience, product use case, content quality, and the creator's ability to make the product feel credible—not just follower count.

Seeding and follow-up

Track the handoff from outreach to product acceptance to posted content. A program cannot learn if samples disappear into a spreadsheet with no owner or follow-through.

Commission and offer strategy

Set creator incentives in the context of product margin, category competition, and the specific product being promoted. Use the profit calculator before setting an offer that cannot scale profitably.

Briefing and creator support

Give creators the product context, important guardrails, and useful angles without turning their content into a script. The aim is better native content, not a pile of identical posts.

How the program runs

1. Diagnose product and offer fit

Start with the hero products, buyer problem, margin, inventory, listing quality, and the product story a creator can demonstrate clearly.

2. Recruit and activate creators

Run targeted sourcing, outreach, sample coordination, and onboarding so qualified creators receive the information and product they need to create useful content.

3. Read the performance signal

Review creator output, content response, shop conversion, and affiliate-attributed results to separate activity from actual commercial progress.

4. Retain, reactivate, and scale

Keep working with the creators and angles showing real fit, re-engage promising relationships, improve the offer where needed, and coordinate paid support only after the organic evidence earns it.

When managed affiliate support is the right fit

This service fits consumer brands with a product ready to sell, reliable fulfilment, enough margin to support creator economics, and a team ready to move quickly on product or brand approvals. It can work as a focused affiliate engagement or as part of full TikTok Shop management when the shop, listings, creators, content, and paid work need to move together.
